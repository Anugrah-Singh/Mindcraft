# IoT SkillsBench Master Reference

This meta-skill provides comprehensive knowledge and benchmarks for embedded and IoT development spanning:
- **Frameworks:** Arduino, ESP-IDF, Zephyr, FreeRTOS
- **Microcontrollers:** ESP32, ESP32-S3, ATmega328P, nRF52840, STM32
- **Protocols & Peripherals:** I2C, SPI, UART, GPIO, PWM, ADC, Timer, RTC
- **Sensors & Actuators:** MPU6050, BMP180, VL53L0X, INA219, WS2812B, OLED SSD1306, and more.

Refer to the individual skill directories in .agents/skills/ or ~/.gemini/antigravity-cli/skills/ for detailed hardware register maps, driver initialization patterns, timing constraints, and troubleshooting tips.
